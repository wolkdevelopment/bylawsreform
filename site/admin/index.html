Access

The plan opens only with this code. People type it on the front page, and capital letters, spaces and punctuation don't matter. Changing it signs everyone out until they enter the new one. Leave it blank to close the plan to everyone.

Requests for the code

“Email the code” opens your email app with a message ready to send. Mark the request done after you send it.

Settings

Changes take effect right away. No redeploy needed.

Your replies to comments show on the public page as “Reply from” and this name, for example “Reply from the author” or “Reply from Brian.”

With “Show right away,” a comment still waits for you if it has a possible threat, strong language or a slur, an email address, phone number or street address, a link, code or HTML, mostly capital letters, a long run of one character, or mostly another alphabet. The person is told a moderator will read it first.

“Per person” means per internet connection. Everyone on the same Wi-Fi, like a meeting room, shares one. Raise the per-person limits before you ask a room full of people to comment. Days and hours are rolling: the last 24 hours and the last 60 minutes.
